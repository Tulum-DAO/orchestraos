## 1. Executive Identity Summary

The official brand mark of **Tulum DAO** (the publisher) and **OrchestraOS** (the product), also used for the **Build-a-thon** (the event), is derived from **El Castillo de Zamá** (*The Fortress of Dawn*), the coastal Mayan temple perched above the Caribbean Sea in Quintana Roo.

## 2. Core Architectural Components

1. **Upper Temple Shrine (*El Templo Superior*):**
   - Flat lintel roof with classic Maya projecting double cornice molding.
   - **Tri-Model Portico (3 Doorways):** Divided by two monolithic columns representing sovereign agent runtimes (**Claude, Gemini, Codex**).
2. **Flanking Lower Shrines (*Los Santuarios Laterales*):**
   - Stepped wings flanking the central chamber, capturing authentic temple hierarchy.
3. **Grand Ceremonial Staircase (*La Gran Escalinata*) & Balustrades (*Alfardas*):**
   - Imposing central ascending stairway with sloping stone balustrades dividing the stepped platforms.
4. **Flush Outer Ring Union:**
   - 12-meter coastal limestone cliff and turquoise Caribbean reef basin expand **flush against the inside wall of the outer portal ring** ($R = 204\text{ px}$), removing floating gaps.
5. **Morning Sun of Zamá (*K'iin*):**
   - Solitary golden sun orb rising in the East.
